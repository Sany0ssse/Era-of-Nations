# Era of Nations

![Era of Nations](thumbnail.png)

**Version 0.1.0-demo.3**
Build: 0.1.0-demo.3-20261013-6725dc23d26b

Lead a modern nation through diplomacy, economic pressure and international cooperation. Era of Nations is a standalone development fork for Hearts of Iron IV 1.19.x, expanding the inherited modern-world setting with more ways to negotiate and manage your country's commitments.

## More choices between war and peace

- Country-to-country diplomacy: establish relations, organise diplomatic missions and use bilateral consultations directly from a country's diplomacy screen.
- Defensive alliances and international agreements: proposals, acceptance, obligations and withdrawal, with national restrictions respected.
- Peace negotiations: either side of a war can open talks. Explore a peace without concessions, an agreed payment, a temporary agreement with mutual withdrawal, or a territorial conference where the conflict allows it.
- Economic cooperation: electricity contracts, investment offers, financial assistance and debt arrangements with tracked terms and responses.

## Institutions, people and resources

- European Union membership, accession procedures, institutional appointments, votes and withdrawal. A dedicated EU button gives current members direct access to the panel.
- Labour migration and refugee reception with capacity, integration, voluntary return and civilian relief funding. Receiving people brings responsibilities as well as opportunities.
- Finite uranium deposits, mining capacity, fuel processing and civilian fuel contracts. Processing facilities use material rather than creating it from nothing.
- Missile research and launch corrections, regimental artillery support and an in-game division design guide.
- A calmer graphite and navy interface, clearer national focus states and more accessible country interactions.

## Install and play

1. Subscribe and wait for Steam to finish downloading the update.
2. Restart the launcher and enable only **Era of Nations - Multiplayer Demo**. Disable the local Era of Nations copy and separate versions of the source mods.
3. Start a new test campaign to explore the new systems.
4. For multiplayer, every player needs the same game version, mod update and main-menu checksum.

**This is a free experimental playtest build.** Mechanics use game rules and engine limitations to represent real-world processes; they do not reproduce every legal or economic detail. AI behaviour and performance are still being improved. Source tests and selected in-game scenarios have been checked, while long campaigns, save compatibility and two-client multiplayer still need further playtesting. No general FPS improvement is promised.

To report a problem, include your game version and checksum, countries, campaign date, the action you took and what happened. For multiplayer, include both players' checksums and any desync message.
